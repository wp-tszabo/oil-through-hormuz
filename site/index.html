Methodology

The headline number is not modelled, smoothed or adjusted. It is the most recent quarterly average EIA has published, shown as published, with the period it covers stated next to it.

We do not currently publish a daily figure. We would like to, and the reason we don’t is worth stating plainly: the sources that do track this strait daily are all commercially licensed or otherwise closed to republication, so the best cadence legally available to us is quarterly. Estimating a single day from a quarterly average is possible, but during the disruption that began in early 2026 the honest error range on such an estimate would be wider than the number itself — roughly one and a half to seven million barrels per day. A figure that uncertain, printed under a date, would mislead more than it informs. If flows return to a steady pattern, a daily estimate becomes meaningful and we will publish one — always with its range shown, never as a bare number.

The full working method, including the sources we checked and rejected on licensing grounds and the back-test behind that error range, is in the project repository, which is public.
