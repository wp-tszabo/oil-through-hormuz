Frequently asked questions

Is the number on this site a live measurement?

No. Nobody publishes a free, daily, direct measurement of oil flow through the Strait of Hormuz. The figure on this site is a model estimate, built from quarterly and monthly official data plus publicly reported signals, and it always carries an uncertainty range. It is never a substitute for a real measurement, and we say so on every page that shows it.

How is the estimate calculated?

It starts from the most recent official quarterly figure (EIA’s Global Energy Security Data), then adjusts between releases using higher-frequency signals we have cleared for use — see the full, specific method on our Sources & methodology page. We do not use satellite or tanker-tracking data; that is explicitly outside the model.

Why does the figure show a range instead of one precise number?

Because a single number would overstate how confident the model actually is. The range reflects how far the model’s past estimates have been from the official figures once they were published, back-tested openly on our Sources & methodology page. The range is never narrowed to make the figure look more precise than the evidence supports, and it widens further whenever the model is extrapolating beyond the period it has been tested against.

How accurate has this estimate been?

We score every past estimate against the official data once it is published and publish the result either way, including the misses — see our estimate history page for the full day-by-day record and our Sources & methodology page for how each quarter scored against the actual figure.

Where does the data behind the estimate come from?

Primarily the U.S. Energy Information Administration’s quarterly Global Energy Security Data release, a U.S. government public-domain source, supplemented by other officially published statistics and publicly reported facts that we cite individually. Every input is listed, dated and sourced on our Sources & methodology page; nothing is used without checking its licence first.

How often does the figure update?

The page refreshes daily. The underlying anchor data is quarterly, so most days’ changes come from the model’s own pacing adjustments rather than new official data — a new quarterly release, when one lands, is the biggest single move the figure makes.

Can I get this data as a feed or download?

Yes. Our own daily estimate history is available as JSON, CSV and an Atom feed from the Estimate history page — always our own modelled figures, never a re-hosting of a source we do not have redistribution rights to.
