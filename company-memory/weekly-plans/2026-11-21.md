## Owner amendment: challenger model (2026-11-22)

**Why.** Research's own §81.8 finding is that the estimator lags turning
points: it freezes the anchor quarter's transit share, and in both
disrupted transitions the true share moved much faster than GPCI (overshoot
of ~80% on the fall, undershoot of ~30% on the recovery; share 0.411 → 0.593
in one quarter). That is enough evidence to build a fix and test it now.
Waiting for 4Q26 to "score H1" leaves a known bias on the live site for
three months.

**What to do this week:**

1. **Build a challenger model (v2).** Same anchor, but the transit share
   is no longer frozen between releases: it adjusts from the faster
   evidence we have. That includes UKMTO's weekly transit counts, PortWatch
   daily tanker counts (tier 3, up to the stall), and confirmed tier-2
   signals from `signal-log.md` (closures, attacks, escorts, insurer moves,
   loading suspensions). Research designs it; keep it simple and explain
   every parameter.
2. **Back-test it now, in hindsight.** Score v1 and v2 on every transition
   we have (1Q26 → 2Q26, 2Q26 → 3Q26, and the calm 2025 quarters), using
   only data that would have been available at the time. Report both
   errors side by side. With only two disrupted transitions, say plainly
   how thin that is and size v2's band accordingly. No band narrower than
   the evidence supports (category 4 still applies).
3. **Test H3 now.** UKMTO's own weekly transit counts are a primary
   source, so the ~19% within-November decline is a confirmed signal under
   the 2026-11-16 rules. Put it through v2 and report what it implies for
   today's figure. If the evidence doesn't support moving the figure, say
   specifically why. "Context only" is not enough.
4. **Run v2 in shadow every day from now on.** Record v1's and v2's daily
   predictions, dated and frozen, in `methodology.md` or a data file, so
   4Q26 scores both honestly when it lands.
5. **CEO decides this week.** If v2 beats v1 on the hindsight back-test
   and is no worse in calm periods, switch the published figure to v2
   under methodology authority (PR + rubric, plain "what changed and why"
   in the PR and on `sources.html`). If it doesn't, keep v1 and record the
   measured reason. Either way, the decision is made this week, not
   deferred to 4Q26.

**Rule going forward:** a confirmed signal (two independent sources, or
one primary) must be able to move the published estimate through the model.
If every signal is logged as "context, no change", the signal log isn't
doing its job; flag that in the cycle report.
