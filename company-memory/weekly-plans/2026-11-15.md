**Objectives**

- **Baseline (Research, this week).** Write down normal Hormuz traffic:
  calm-period volume and its oil/LNG split, seasonality, typical
  week-to-week variance, and how traffic behaved in past disruptions (2019
  tanker attacks, 2024 Red Sea diversions, any 2025–26 episodes). Use EIA
  history plus cited reporting. Output: a baseline section in
  `methodology.md` with numbers and sources.
- **Prediction model v1 (Research → CEO decision).** Turn current signals
  into a predicted level with a band. Start `company-memory/signal-log.md`
  and fill it with what is known now about the strait's current state from
  news, X, YouTube, maritime advisories (UKMTO, JMIC, MARAD), insurer
  war-risk notices and producer/port announcements. Each entry is dated,
  linked, graded for reliability and marked confirmed or unconfirmed.
  Propose how the signals adjust the current estimator, back-tested on past
  disruptions where possible. The CEO decides adoption under methodology
  authority and publishes through the normal PR + rubric path.
- **Validate against daily data now (tier 3, owner decision 2026-11-16).**
  Pull IMF PortWatch's Hormuz transit series, plus any other reachable
  daily or weekly dataset, and score the model's published history against
  it. That gives the first real error measurement at better than quarterly
  resolution. Use the findings to fix the model. Internal only: never
  publish their numbers or make them the anchor. **Order matters:** freeze
  the 3Q26 prediction (next item) *before* looking at PortWatch data for
  July–September 2026, or the EIA score stops being an honest test.
- **Freeze a prediction for 3Q26 before EIA publishes.** Record the model's
  3Q26 prediction (point and band) in `methodology.md` now, dated, so it
  can be scored honestly when EIA lands. When it lands: score first, then
  re-anchor and improve.
- **G3 traffic (CEO, every cycle).** Read GoatCounter with
  `GOATCOUNTER_API_TOKEN`, record the date range and total hits in
  `metrics.md`, and compare with the prior period. If it can't be read,
  record it as unmeasured with the error.
- **G3 growth action this week (Build, CEO review).** Add a **"What's
  happening in the strait"** section: a short, dated, cited list of this
  week's confirmed signals from `signal-log.md`, rewritten in our own words
  with links, never copied. Also add the site's prediction-vs-actual record
  once one exists. This gives visitors a reason to come back, and gives
  search engines fresh, original content. Review the hit count next cycle.
